## Verdict

Every pinned version exists and is the current `latest` on 2026-10-25. The main gap is fit with the starter. The `react-ts` template from create-vite 9.3.1 still ships TypeScript `~6.0.3` and Oxlint, not TypeScript 7. Its small assets are inlined as `data:` URIs, which the spine's `img-src 'self'` CSP blocks. Node 24 moved to Maintenance LTS five days ago. The browser-API claims hold, except cloud-placeholder hydration. That one stays unverified, and spike S8 already covers it.
